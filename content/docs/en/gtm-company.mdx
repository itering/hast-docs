<h2 id="intelligence">Inspect company analysis</h2>

Project intelligence organizes products, customers, pricing, social accounts, and competitors. Rerun analysis after website changes and check sources against the actual business.

<h2 id="documents">Read project documents</h2>

Documents are generated from the latest company analysis as four kinds of Markdown:

| Document | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Product Information | Product facts |
| Brand Voice | Brand expression |
| Competitor Analysis | Competitor research |
| Marketing Strategy | Marketing approach |

Open documents to read or copy Markdown for reuse. If none exist, run company analysis before generating documents.

<h2 id="review">Verify facts before writing</h2>

Check names, feature scope, customers, and prices. Generated analysis may contain inferences; verify it against the product before publishing articles or posts.

To update content sources, refresh the website and company analysis, then regenerate affected documents or drafts. A draft's creation date is not the date of the latest source information.
