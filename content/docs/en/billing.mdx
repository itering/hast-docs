<h2 id="plan">Check product access</h2>

Open the account center from the avatar menu and inspect the current Profile's plan. For locked Task Agent or GTM actions, confirm the Profile, plan validity, and allowances.

Prices, available models, and limits follow the current account-center display. Team and personal billing scopes differ; check the Profile before purchasing.

<h2 id="usage">Inspect usage</h2>

Use the usage page to inspect the current period. Compare the same period and Profile before and after tasks when checking costs. Check model-account authorization separately from Hast plan status.

Team members needing more usage should use the team's request and approval flow. Contact the team billing administrator about allowance issues.

<h2 id="team">Team seats</h2>

Administrators manage members and invitations in the workspace. Pending invitations reserve seats. Removing a member does not automatically reduce purchased seats or issue a refund.

To reduce seats, change the next period's quantity. It cannot be lower than the seats needed by active members and pending invitations.

<h2 id="renew">Renewal and cancellation</h2>

Check renewal state and period end in plans and billing. Cancellation at period end keeps paid access through that date. Before confirming changes, read their effective time and charges in the UI.
