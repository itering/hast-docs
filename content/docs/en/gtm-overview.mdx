Hast GTM is the marketing and growth module within Hast AIP. A website becomes a project whose company and product context supports research, GEO, technical analysis, and content tasks.

After you enter a website, GTM reads it, organizes company and product information, and provides performance, SEO, AI brand mention, and traffic analysis. You can also generate X posts and article drafts.

<h2 id="uses">Marketing use cases</h2>

After launching a website, teams need to understand how the product is perceived and identify site issues before deciding what content to add. GTM puts these tasks in one project to help plan the next step.

- Organize positioning, target customers, and competitors for research and writing.
- Inspect search, website performance, and AI brand mentions to identify questions worth investigating.
- Prepare articles and social drafts from project context, review them, and use them in your own channels.

<h2 id="context">Shared context for research and content</h2>

Project context includes website information, company profiles, and documents, reducing repeated explanations of the product, audience, and brand voice.

After initialization, check the facts. When positioning or site content changes, refresh context before generating new analysis or drafts. See [project management](gtm-projects) and [company analysis](gtm-company).

<h2 id="workspace">Inside a project</h2>

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Company and website information | Products, brand voice, competitors, marketing documents |
| SEO and Performance | PageSpeed results for mobile and desktop |
| GEO | Buyer questions and brand mentions in model answers |
| Traffic | Website traffic after Google Analytics authorization |
| Agents Feed | X posts and article drafts for review |
| Growth Agent | Ask about the project or start supported operations |
| Run activity | Background task status and events |

<h2 id="start">Start with a website</h2>

Follow the [quickstart](gtm-quickstart). Check website and company facts after initialization before running content tasks.

Rerun setup to update website context. New analysis and content require their background jobs to finish; a queued job is not a completed result.

<h2 id="workflow">Act on analysis</h2>

1. Check company, product, and audience information.
2. Choose [Performance](gtm-performance) for speed and SEO, [GEO](gtm-geo) for AI mentions, or [Traffic](gtm-traffic) for visits.
3. Generate [articles](gtm-articles) or [X drafts](gtm-x), then verify facts, links, and brand language.
4. After publishing or changing your site, inspect changes in later analyses.

Traffic requires Google Analytics. Review generated content; publish X drafts using the provided composer link. PageSpeed reports findings, while website fixes must be made in your own site project.

<h2 id="agent">Operate through Growth Agent</h2>

Use [Growth Agent](gtm-growth-agent) to ask questions while reviewing context and start supported refresh, analysis, or content operations. Check background progress and output in run history.
