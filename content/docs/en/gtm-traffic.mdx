<h2 id="connect">Connect an account</h2>

1. Open Traffic in the project.
2. Choose `Connect` on the Google Analytics card.
3. Complete Google authorization and return to GTM.
4. Verify that the connected account appears.

`Unavailable` means this deployment cannot start authorization yet; its maintainer needs to check connection configuration.

<h2 id="property">Choose a property</h2>

Select the site's property in `Choose GA property` and save. Authorization without selecting a property is not enough to display that site's traffic.

If the property is missing, check whether the authorized Google account has access to it.

<h2 id="sync">Sync and inspect</h2>

Choose `Run Traffic` to refresh data. Wait for synchronization, then view the last 7 or 30 days.

For missing data, check connection state, selected property, and sync results in that order. Blank or failed results do not mean the site had no traffic.
